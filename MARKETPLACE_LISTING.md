### Technical specs

- **Engine:** Blender 4.2+ (tested on 5.2.3 LTS)
- **Renderer:** EEVEE (works in Cycles too)
- **Resolution:** 512×512 previews, 64 samples
- **Node count:** 5–12 nodes per material
- **Textures:** 0 (100% procedural)
- **File size:** ~1.2 MB per .blend
- **Total pack size:** ~3.5 MB (zipped)

### How to use

1. Open any `.blend` file in Blender.
2. The material is assigned to a cylinder and a sphere (test geometry).
3. Copy the material to your own mesh: right-click → *Copy Material* → select target → *Paste Material*.
4. Adjust `Scale` on the Texture Coordinate / Mapping node to resize the pattern.
5. Tweak `Roughness` / `Metallic` on the Principled BSDF for your scene.

### Why procedural?

- **Infinite resolution** — no blurry textures at close-up.
- **Tiny file size** — no 4K PNGs eating your project.
- **Easy to tweak** — change one slider, get a new look.
